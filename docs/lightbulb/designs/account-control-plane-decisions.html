Lightbulb Account Control Plane

Decision artifact — 2026-06-20 — fork OpenCode, add loop/goal orchestration and bidirectional artifact flow.
Decision 1

Fork OpenCode

Use OpenCode for TUI/session/diff/provider substrate. Lightbulb owns the account work graph.

Decision 2

SQLite v0

Add separate Lightbulb tables beside OpenCode's schema. Keep future Postgres/server mode possible.

Decision 3

Artifacts are first-class

Files, repos, diffs, reports, plans, logs, and generated assets get IDs, lineage, producers, consumers, and status.

Decision 4

Delegate before context rot

Parents orchestrate. Fresh child workers execute. Parent receives summary + artifact handles, not raw transcript sludge.
